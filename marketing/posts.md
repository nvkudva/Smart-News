## LinkedIn (attach smartnews-launch-4x5.mp4)

I got tired of reading the same news story six times.

One rate decision, one election result, one outage, and every outlet runs its own version. Half of it is the same wire copy with a new headline. So I built Smart-News, a reader that groups every report of the same event into one story and has a model write one neutral summary across all of them.

You get one summary per event, plus one line each on how left, centre and right outlets framed it, and a link to every original. Under the hood it reads 83 outlets, counts newsrooms rather than URLs (five sites carrying the same wire copy count once), and won't summarise anything until 2 independent outlets carry it.

It refreshes every 30 minutes, has no ads and nothing to sign in to. Not perfect, the summaries are only as neutral as the instructions I give the model, but I now read the news once a day instead of six times.

Try it now and tell me what you think.
